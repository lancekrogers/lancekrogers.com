# Content Strategy for lancekrogers.com

## Content Goals

### Primary Objectives
1. **Establish Personal Brand**: Position Lance as a recognized expert in blockchain and AI engineering
2. **Showcase Technical Expertise**: Demonstrate depth of knowledge through projects and writing
3. **Build Professional Network**: Connect with peers, potential collaborators, and opportunities
4. **Thought Leadership**: Share insights and contribute to technical community discussions

### Target Audience
- **Primary**: Senior engineers, CTOs, and technical decision makers
- **Secondary**: Blockchain and AI community members
- **Tertiary**: Potential consulting clients and collaborators

## Content Tone and Voice

### Technical Authority
- Demonstrate deep expertise without being intimidating
- Use specific examples and metrics
- Share real-world challenges and solutions

### Approachable Expert
- Personal anecdotes and learning stories
- Acknowledge failures and learnings
- Show curiosity and continuous learning

### Community Focus
- Highlight contributions to open source
- Mention mentoring and community building
- Show collaboration and knowledge sharing
