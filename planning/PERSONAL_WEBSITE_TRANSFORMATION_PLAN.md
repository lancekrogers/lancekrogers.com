# Personal Website Transformation Plan
## From Blockhead Consulting to lancekrogers.com

### Current State Analysis

**Current Identity: Blockhead Consulting**
- Business-focused consulting website
- Tagline: "Protocol-level blockchain & AI engineering"
- Subtitle: "Ship faster, spend less, no vendor lock in"
- Primary branding: Green cyberpunk aesthetic with "Blockhead" logos
- Structure: Services-focused with hourly rates, packages, problems/solutions

**Current Content Structure:**
- Hero: Company name with glitch effects
- Problems: Client challenges and solutions
- Services: Crypto Infrastructure + AI/LLM Consulting with rates
- Work Experience: Detailed professional background
- Contact: Business consultation booking

### Target State: Personal Website (lancekrogers.com)

**New Identity: Lance Rogers - Personal Portfolio**
- Personal brand focused on showcasing expertise and experience
- Professional but approachable tone
- Emphasis on projects, experience, and thought leadership
- Potential for consulting leads but not primary focus

**Content Strategy Recommendations:**

1. **Hero Section**
   - Name: "Lance Rogers" (not company name)
   - Tagline: "Software Engineer & Systems Architect"
   - Subtitle: "Building scalable blockchain and AI infrastructure"
   - Focus on personal brand, not business services

2. **About Section**
   - Personal story and background
   - Technical expertise and passions
   - Current focus areas and interests
   - More personal, less corporate

3. **Work/Projects Section** 
   - Showcase of notable projects and contributions
   - Open source work (Guild Framework, Claude Code Go SDK)
   - Professional experience highlights
   - Case studies of interesting technical challenges

4. **Blog Section**
   - Technical writing and insights
   - Industry commentary
   - Project deep-dives
   - Personal learnings and experiences

5. **Contact Section**
   - Simple contact form
   - Social media links
   - "Let's connect" messaging vs. "Book consultation"

### Visual Identity Changes

**Remove:**
- Blockhead Consulting logos and branding
- Green cyberpunk color scheme (or tone it down)
- Business-focused imagery
- Service packages and hourly rates

**Add:**
- Personal name prominence
- Cleaner, more professional aesthetic
- Focus on projects and achievements
- Social proof through work and contributions

### Technical Implementation Strategy

**Phase 1: Content Transformation**
1. Update site.yml with personal branding
2. Rewrite hero content and messaging
3. Transform services section to projects/skills
4. Update about content for personal story
5. Modify contact messaging

**Phase 2: Visual Rebrand**
1. Replace logos and brand imagery
2. Adjust color scheme for personal brand
3. Update favicon and social media images
4. Refine typography and spacing

**Phase 3: Structure Optimization**
1. Reorganize navigation for personal site
2. Add portfolio/projects showcase
3. Enhance blog for thought leadership
4. Optimize for personal SEO

### Key Decisions Needed

1. **Visual Style**: Keep cyberpunk aesthetic or move to cleaner professional look?
2. **Color Scheme**: New colors or refined version of current green theme?
3. **Logo/Branding**: Personal logo, initials, or text-only branding?
4. **Consulting Element**: Completely remove or keep subtle "available for consulting"?
5. **Domain**: Confirm this will be deployed to lancekrogers.com
6. **Content Tone**: Technical expert, approachable engineer, or thought leader focus?

### Success Metrics

- Clear personal brand identity
- Showcase of technical expertise and projects
- Professional but approachable presentation
- Effective portfolio for potential opportunities
- Strong foundation for thought leadership content
